# Business Recommendations

## Project Objective

Analyze retail sales, profit, regional performance, product profitability, and discounting patterns to identify opportunities for improving business performance.

## Key Findings

- **Regional performance:** The West region generated the highest sales and profit in the regional analysis. Central had the lowest profit margin, at approximately 7.92%.
- **Product profitability:** Tables generated a loss of approximately $17,305, while Bookcases generated a loss of approximately $3,473.
- **Discount patterns:** Transactions in the above-30% discount band had an aggregate profit margin of approximately -37.28% in the analyzed dataset.

## Recommendations

1. Review the pricing and cost structure of loss-making sub-categories, especially Tables and Bookcases.
2. Evaluate high-discount transactions and consider testing approval thresholds for large discounts.
3. Investigate why Central's profit margin is lower than those of other regions.
4. Track sales, profit, and profit margin together to avoid prioritizing revenue at the expense of profitability.

## Limitations

These are descriptive findings from a sample retail dataset. The observed relationship between discounts and profit does not establish causation. Further analysis should account for product mix, regional differences, costs, and transaction characteristics before making policy changes.

One record had a missing Profit value in the imported dataset. The missing value should be documented and handled consistently when reproducing the analysis.
